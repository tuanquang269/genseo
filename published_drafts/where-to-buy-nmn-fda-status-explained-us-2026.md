## Why NMN Disappeared From Some US Listings

The confusion began with the FDA's 2022 drug-preclusion position. In letters concerning NMN notifications, the agency said NMN was excluded from the dietary-supplement definition under section 201(ff)(3)(B)(ii) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. That is a classification question: the statute can exclude an ingredient from the supplement definition when it has been authorized for investigation as a drug and the statutory conditions apply.

The 2022 position did not establish that every NMN product was unsafe. It also did not turn an NMN listing into an FDA-approved medicine. It changed how the ingredient could be marketed under the supplement rules, which helps explain why some marketplaces and sellers removed or restricted listings after the letters became public. Marketplace decisions are separate from FDA classification, so an old search result is not a reliable current legal answer.

The FDA's May 16, 2022 letter to SyncoZymes acknowledged an NMN new-dietary-ingredient notification. Later 2022 letters took the exclusion position for another NMN notification. Read the original records rather than relying on a retailer's “FDA approved” badge: a notification acknowledgment is not product approval, and an exclusion letter is not a safety verdict.

## Is NMN Legal to Buy in the US Now?

Yes, NMN is not currently excluded from the dietary-supplement definition based on the FDA's September 29, 2025 response to a citizen petition. The FDA said it was changing its interpretation of the race-to-market provision and concluded that NMN had been marketed as a dietary supplement in the United States before the relevant drug-investigation authorization date.

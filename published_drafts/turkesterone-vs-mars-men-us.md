## What does the human turkesterone study show?

**The cited trial did not find significant between-group improvements in the outcomes it measured.** It also did not test either finished product in this comparison.

The study's small sample and short duration limit what can be concluded. A null result in this setting is not proof that every possible effect is absent in every population. But uncertainty does not reverse the finding into a positive result. The appropriate response is to keep the claim narrow.

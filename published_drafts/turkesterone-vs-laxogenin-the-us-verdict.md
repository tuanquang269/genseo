## What does the human turkesterone study actually show?

**The acute human trial did not establish that turkesterone builds muscle.** Researchers examined short-term biological and metabolic measures, not months of training progress or a direct contest against laxogenin.

That distinction matters because a supplement explanation can slide from “researchers measured a growth-related marker” to “the supplement increases growth.” Those are different statements. The paper discusses nonsignificant trends and calls for further investigation. Those observations can help researchers choose a future study. They do not provide a reliable result to promise a shopper. Nor does this small, acute experiment settle every question about longer-term use.

<h2 data-mce-fragment="1" class="heading-2 ace-line old-record-id-Oaohd3vzRoOq4SxhVMiuNp5OsBg">Shilajit's European History and Ayurvedic Origins</h2>

<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-SDZidrHOIoa0hkxTq7xuGqk4sNg">With firm origins in the East, the ancient Indians and other cultures revered it as a powerful medicine, with recorded history making detailed mention of this potent substance for more than 3000 years!</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" data-type="image" class="image-uploaded gallery old-record-id-BYacdcOXAoAjmRxm2icuOuogsJm"></div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-NeWtdZ0Yoon8vGxExwBuAv3Vswd">Found in the caves and crevices of very high mountain slopes (10 000-16 000 ft) throughout India, China, Central Asia, Iran and a few other Arabian countries; the indigenous people of these areas are all very well acquainted with Shilajit. Other names for this substance include Shilajeet, Silaras, or Shilajatu.</div>
<h3 data-mce-fragment="1" class="heading-3 ace-line old-record-id-NfRPdtjzIoDWYpx4APBukuAJsJe">Shilajit in Ancient Ayurveda</h3>
<p><img style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" src="https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0810/1563/6283/files/Untitled_design_18_480x480.png?v=1701086032" alt="Bowl of dark resin surrounded by herbs, spices, and oils"></p>

<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-EVLAdmrUCowi8DxsugGu0woosBb">Some of the oldest records of this medicine stem back approximately 5000 years to India and the Himalayan Mountains, which has led many to believe that this is where Shilajit originates from. Still today, the best quality Shilajit comes from the Himalayan mountains and the Altai mountains, both of which are speculated to be between 300 and 500 million years old!</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-G3EEd2M63oJo8OxeHcluH6vBsAg">Ancient Hindu and Vedic texts - such as the <em data-mce-fragment="1">Charaka Samhita and Sushrut Samhita</em> that later formed the basis of Ayurveda - explicitly outline how to prepare Shilajit, what herbs to mix it with and when it is appropriate to administer it to treat a whole host of diseases. Some of these texts even make divine, spiritual and mythological reference to Shilajit, stating that it came from the Hindu god Shiva.</div>

<h3 data-mce-fragment="1" class="heading-3 ace-line old-record-id-Eu7BdNrFyozWlkxMVSTu6ST1sNb">Moomijo and the Widespread Eastern Origins of Shilajit</h3>
<p><img style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" src="https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0810/1563/6283/files/Untitled_design_28_480x480.png?v=1701087024" alt="Person in red carrying a load toward a snow-covered mountain"></p>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" data-type="image" class="image-uploaded gallery old-record-id-UCZedH1mUo89JbxQB57u0oA4spb"></div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-L95FdJ4xAoYGWgxRdutuXNccsae">There is another substance nearly identical to Shilajit that was discovered by the folk people living in the Altai Mountains and other Eurasian ranges such as the Caucasus Mountains.</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-EjUvd3uXkoNb3XxDINnubgQgsD8">This type of Shilajit is known as Mumijo, Mumiyo, Mumie, momio, myemu or mummy. The name is a variation of the Greek word mumie, which means "to save or preserve the body".</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-H0fNdbHCroM9FqxlmuluVfHWsFe">There is very little difference between Mumiyo and Shilajit, aside from the fact that the chemical composition differs slightly and that Himalayan Shilajit is seen to be older, rarer and slightly more potent. Many people use the word Shilajit and Mumijo interchangeably, as their properties are virtually identical.</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-WCt6d6gSGoVJrxxhLMXuIYHRsKg">Mumiyo has been venerated countless times in Russian, Turkish, Sumerian, Egyptian, Persian and Arabic texts too.</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-GV5PdncP5obtBYxEF12uM1IMsWb">In one such text titled <em data-mce-fragment="1">'The Form of Climates' (Mahzanul-Adwiya)</em> by Alhakim Alyavi, a story is described in which Mumijo was discovered in a mountainous cave by the Sultan's guard. He placed a sentry outside the cave to guard it like treasure and once a year, his servants would collect it.</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-LMgqdj2IQoUReExGzpDugeQfsnh">Moreover, famous doctors, scientists and philosophers knew about Shilajit; such as Avicenna, Paracleus and Buruni, who have had a large impact on shaping ideas within society today.</div>
<div data-mce-fragment="1" data-type="image" class="image-uploaded gallery old-record-id-PCgGdlDqJoHKplxosHqugJyesCd"></div>

<div data-mce-fragment="1" class="ace-line ace-line old-record-id-QrkMdRLJFoN41uxQtwsu59Pgs0c">Amongst those who knew, Mumiyo and all related substances were regarded as the closest thing to the legendary Fountain of Youth.</div>

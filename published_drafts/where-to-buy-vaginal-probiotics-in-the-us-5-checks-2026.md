### How much do vaginal probiotics cost per month?

### What should a vaginal probiotic label show?

Look for the genus, species, and strain when provided; CFU per clearly stated serving; serving size; storage directions; expiry or lot information; allergens; and the manufacturer or seller. If a listing makes a claim that the pouch does not support, ask for clarification before ordering.

### Is buying vaginal probiotics online safe?

Online buying is safer when the seller is identifiable, the product page matches the pouch, the seal and expiry are clear, returns are explained, and the product is stored as directed. Avoid listings that promise to diagnose or cure symptoms. Seek clinical advice for persistent, painful, or concerning symptoms.

<p>You can take NMN while drinking coffee every day. No human trial has shown that coffee cancels NMN or that you must separate them. Do not stir NMN powder into hot coffee. AgeAura Pure NMN + TMG is a capsule, and a plain-water study did not test coffee. In the US, swallow the capsules as the label directs.</p>

<h2>Can you take NMN while drinking coffee every day?</h2>
<p>Yes. A daily coffee habit is not a reason, on current human evidence, to skip NMN or to wait a set number of hours. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-021-00442-4">Liao and colleagues</a> randomized 48 amateur runners for six weeks to 300 mg, 600 mg, or 1,200 mg of NMN a day, or placebo, plus training. The paper does not test coffee. The <a href="https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much">FDA has cited 400 mg of caffeine a day</a> as an amount not generally associated with negative effects for most adults, about two to three 12-fluid-ounce cups of coffee, and it also says sensitivity varies. That caffeine note is not an NMN dose.</p>

<h2>Can you put NMN powder in coffee?</h2>
<p>Not as a labeled use of this product. AgeAura Pure NMN + TMG is a capsule. The 2026-10-08 pack-shot whitelist lists the lines 60, Capsules, and 30 Servings, and it does not list a milligram amount, so this block states none. <a href="https://doi.org/10.19540/j.cnki.cjcmm.20230905.301">A 2023 HPLC study of NMN in plain water</a> found that high temperature and pH drive the breakdown. At room temperature the authors report t0.9 of 95.58 hours and a half-life of 860.26 hours. Strong acid or strong alkali sped the loss. That study did not use coffee, so a loss percentage for a hot cup is unverified. Swallow the capsules. For powder as a format, read the capsules-versus-powder article linked below.</p>

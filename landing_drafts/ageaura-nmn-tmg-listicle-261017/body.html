01

NMN changes a blood marker

NMN is a precursor your body can use in NAD+ biosynthesis. Human trials have measured whether supplementation changes blood NAD-related markers.

That is a biochemical outcome, not a promise of more energy, better mood, or longer life.

Human RCT · Huang et al., Front Aging 2022

02

Results depend on the outcome

In a 60-day trial of 66 adults aged 40 to 65, the NMN group’s blood NAD+/NADH rose 38% from baseline.

Placebo rose 14.3%. The study measured blood markers and selected health measures, not a universal feeling of vitality.

Human RCT · Huang et al., Front Aging 2022

03

TMG has a different research role

TMG is another name for betaine. Its human research includes testing homocysteine, a marker related to methylation, rather than testing NAD+ levels.

That distinction matters: a finding about betaine alone cannot establish that adding it improves the response to NMN.

Human RCT, TMG only · Olthof et al., J Nutr 2003

04

TMG evidence is separate

TMG, also called betaine, has been studied on its own in healthy adults. One trial tested daily betaine against placebo for six weeks.

That study reported lower fasting homocysteine. It did not test NMN, NAD+, or this two-ingredient formula.

05

A rationale is not proof

NMN and TMG are often discussed together because NMN research examines NAD-related metabolism while betaine research examines methylation-related markers.

Separate mechanisms can explain a pairing. They cannot establish that the combination improves a result.

Evidence interpretation · separate ingredient studies

06

No combined trial was found

The human studies cited here tested NMN alone or betaine alone. A targeted Europe PMC search found no human trial testing NMN and TMG together.

That is an evidence gap, not evidence that the ingredients cannot be combined or that the formula works.

Literature check · no combined human trial found

07

The studies used daily routines

The cited interventions followed daily routines over weeks, rather than occasional use. That design tells us what researchers actually tested.

For any supplement routine, the practical format is the one a person can follow consistently.

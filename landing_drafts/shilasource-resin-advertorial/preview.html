It's a resin, not a powder

Shilajit is a sticky, tar-like substance traditionally used in Ayurveda. ShilaSource comes as a resin in a jar: you take a small portion and dissolve it in warm water or tea instead of swallowing a capsule.

2

It dissolves in warm water or tea

Follow the directions on the package; it tells you how much to take and how often. Most people dissolve their serving in warm water or tea. Our step-by-step guide covers it: How to take shilajit resin.

3

What the research tested (and didn't)

In a 90-day placebo-controlled trial, healthy men aged 45 to 55 taking purified shilajit twice daily had higher total and free testosterone and DHEAS than the placebo group (Pandit et al., 2016).

In another trial, 63 recreationally active young men took a lower or higher daily amount for 8 weeks. Among the stronger half of participants, the higher-dose group lost less strength after a fatiguing leg workout (Keller et al., 2019).

Both studies used a purified, standardized extract, not ShilaSource resin, so they don't show that our jar will do the same.

We don't claim that. One 90-day study of a purified shilajit extract in men aged 45 to 55 reported higher testosterone than placebo, but that was a different product and a single study.

Can I take it with other supplements or medication?

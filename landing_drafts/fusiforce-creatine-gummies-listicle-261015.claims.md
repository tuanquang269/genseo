## Deliberately omitted

- PDP marketing claims about lab testing, COAs, customer totals, average product ratings, recovery, energy, focus, cognitive effects, purity, and ingredient performance. They were not used as evidence.
- Study dosage amounts in narrative copy. Dosage-with-units is restricted to the verified product and offer cards.
- Any claim that creatine replaces resistance training or guarantees strength, lean-mass, recovery, or other outcomes.
- Product name, link, and product-specific claims from the numbered research findings before the bridge.
- Countdown, scarcity, fake testimonials, expert quotes, and faces.

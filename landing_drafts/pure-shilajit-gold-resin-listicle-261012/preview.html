01

Three formats, three methods

Shilajit is sold as a raw resin you pinch into water, a capsule with a standardized extract inside, or a gummy with shilajit added to a sweetened base.

Each format changes how much fulvic acid and trace mineral content actually reaches the dose, not just how it tastes.

02

Capsule trial raised testosterone

In a placebo-controlled trial of men aged 45 to 55, a purified shilajit capsule taken twice daily for 90 days raised free testosterone 19.14 percent versus baseline.

DHEAS, a related hormone, rose 31.35 percent over the same 90 days. Placebo did not match either change.

Human RCT · Pandit et al., Andrologia 2016

03

No trial tested shilajit gummies

We searched Europe PMC and PubMed for a human trial of a shilajit gummy. We found none.

Gummy formulas also carry less active material per serving than a resin or a standardized capsule, since sugar and gelling agents take up space in the dose.

Literature check · no gummy trial found

04

Resin pilot had no placebo

A 28-day pilot study gave 25 healthy men a daily dose of shilajit resin (exact amount in the chart source below). Leg press strength rose 12.94 percent and grip strength rose 5.73 percent.

The design was open label with no placebo arm, so some of that gain could reflect training familiarity rather than the resin alone.

05

Trials measured different things

The capsule trial measured hormone levels in blood. The resin pilot measured strength and endurance in the gym. Neither trial measured the other's outcome.

That makes format-to-format comparison a guess dressed up as a fact. Read what a study measured before you read what it means.

06

Contamination risk, any format

A 2025 lab survey tested raw shilajit and finished shilajit supplements for thallium, a toxic metal. Trace amounts turned up in both, and the exact levels are in the chart source below.

Some finished products tested higher than the raw material they came from. The survey did not break results out by resin versus capsule versus gummy.

Lab survey · Kamgar et al., BMC Chemistry 2025

07

Ask for third-party lab tests

Since the contamination study above found no safe format, the only control left is testing. Ask a brand for a current batch's heavy-metal lab result before you buy.

A brand that will not produce one is a bigger red flag than the format you pick.

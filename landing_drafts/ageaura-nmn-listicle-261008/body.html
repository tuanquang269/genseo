01

NMN is a NAD+ building block

NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) is a molecule your body can turn into NAD+, a coenzyme your cells use to make energy.

So the real question is simple: does swallowing NMN raise NAD+ in people? Several trials tested exactly that.

02

NAD+ rose 38% in 60 days

In a placebo-controlled trial of 66 adults aged 40 to 65, the NMN group's blood NAD+/NADH was up 11% at day 30 and 38% at day 60.

Placebo rose 14.3% by day 60. Note what was measured: a blood marker, not energy or mood.

Human RCT · Huang et al., Front Aging 2022

03

Faster walking in older adults

In a 12-week trial of older adults, the NMN group finished a 4-meter walk faster than placebo and had higher blood NAD+. Sleep scores improved too.

Honest caveat: the main outcome, a stepping test, showed no difference. Walking and sleep were secondary results in one small study.

Human RCT · Morifuji et al., GeroScience 2024

04

The bigger picture is mixed

A 2025 meta-analysis pooled 12 randomized trials with 513 people. NMN reliably raised blood NAD+.

But most health markers, like fasting glucose and cholesterol, did not beat placebo. The authors warn that NMN's benefits may be exaggerated.

05

Why TMG is paired with NMN

Your body clears leftover nicotinamide partly by methylation. In the walking trial, those methylated by-products rose with NMN.

TMG (betaine) is a methyl donor. In healthy adults, daily betaine lowered homocysteine, a methylation marker, within six weeks (Olthof 2003).

Human RCT (TMG only) · Olthof et al., J Nutr 2003

06

No combined trial yet

Every trial here tested NMN alone or TMG alone. We found no published human trial of the two combined.

So the pairing rests on each ingredient's separate evidence. Unproven as a pair is not the same as unproven at all.

Literature check · no combined NMN + TMG trial found

07

Trials tested daily use

Every trial above dosed people daily for weeks or months (see sources). None tested taking it now and then.

So the best format is the one you will actually take every day.
